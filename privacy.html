Privacy policy — gismo-backup-rclone

Last updated: 2026-10-11

  • Who uses it: only Genius Gismo Limited, for its own backups.
  • What it accesses: the Google Drive scope drive.file only — files that this tool itself created in the company's own Drive account. It cannot see any other files.
  • What it does with them: uploads, lists, downloads and deletes its own backup files. Nothing else.
  • Sharing: no data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No analytics or tracking.
  • Storage: access tokens are kept only on the company's own computer.
  • Removal: access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
